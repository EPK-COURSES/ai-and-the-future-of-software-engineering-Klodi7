# Personal Reflection

While researching this topic, I learned that Artificial Intelligence is changing software engineering much faster than I expected. AI tools are already helping developers write code, automate tasks, and improve efficiency in many companies.

At first, I believed AI might eventually replace many software engineering jobs. After completing this research, my opinion changed. I now think AI will mostly act as a support tool that helps developers work faster instead of replacing them completely.

One thing that surprised me was how important human skills still are. Even if AI can generate code, developers are still responsible for understanding software systems, solving complex problems, and making important decisions.

I am also concerned that some programmers may depend too much on AI tools and stop practicing their own skills. This could create problems related to software quality and security.

On the positive side, AI can remove repetitive tasks and allow developers to focus on more creative and meaningful work. I think this could improve productivity and make software development more efficient.

In the future, I believe software engineers will need to continue learning throughout their careers. Personally, I want to improve my technical knowledge, communication skills, and ability to work with modern technologies so I can adapt successfully to future industry changes.
